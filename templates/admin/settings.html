Google AdSense Approval Guide

Your website is pre-configured with all required pages and metadata for quick Google AdSense approval!

Steps to Get Approved & Start Earning:
  1. Go to Google AdSense and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Sites -> Add Site and enter your domain: https://jobportal-3-123t.onrender.com
  3. Copy your Publisher ID (starts with ca-pub-) and paste it into the left form field above.
  4. Check the Enable Google AdSense checkbox and click Save Settings.
  5. Google will inspect your site within 24-48 hours. Once approved, live Google Ads will automatically start showing and generating revenue!
