Editorial & Content Policy

Our standards for verified job publishing and original analysis

1. Verification Standard

Every job listing posted on Campus to Career undergoes manual verification against official employer career pages, press releases, or official hiring announcements. We strictly refrain from publishing fake or unverified job leads.

2. Original Analysis

We add distinct value to every listing by writing original Campus to Career Analysis sections, including preparation advice, resume recommendations, and breakdown of eligibility criteria.

3. Application Link Integrity

We maintain strict link integrity. All job application buttons point exclusively to official employer domain portals or authorized recruitment ATS systems.
